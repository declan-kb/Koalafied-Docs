# External Resources

Most problems we run into have already been solved by another FRC team. Check these before designing something from scratch. For vendors, see [Vendors](./vendors).

## Community

### Chief Delphi

[Chief Delphi](https://www.chiefdelphi.com) is the main FRC forum. If you have a question, someone has probably asked it here already.

- [Technical](https://www.chiefdelphi.com/c/technical/9): mechanical, electrical, programming and CAD discussion
- [CAD](https://www.chiefdelphi.com/c/technical/cad/68): CAD releases and Onshape help
- [Robot Showcase](https://www.chiefdelphi.com/c/first/robot-showcase/37): robot reveals, often with CAD links
- [Papers](https://www.chiefdelphi.com/c/cd-media-papers/): white papers and calculators

::: tip Searching
The built-in search is weak. Google works better: `site:chiefdelphi.com <your question>`, e.g. `site:chiefdelphi.com elevator cascade vs continuous`.
:::

### The Open Alliance

The Open Alliance is a group of teams that share their whole season as it happens: design decisions, prototypes, CAD and code. Their build threads are in the [Chief Delphi Open Alliance category](https://www.chiefdelphi.com/c/first/open-alliance/89).

Good for:

- Seeing how strong teams go from kickoff to a finished robot
- Finding prototypes and test results for this year's game
- Finding full robot CAD to learn from

### Discord Servers

For quick questions that don't need a full Chief Delphi thread, try the Unofficial FRC Discord or the Onshape FRC community.

## Learning

### FRCDesign.org

[FRCDesign.org](https://frcdesign.org) is where we learn Onshape (see [Onshape Setup](./onshape)). It also has:

- [Learning Course](https://frcdesign.org/learning-course/): Onshape taught through designing robot parts
- [Mechanism Examples](https://frcdesign.org/mechanism-examples/): example CAD for common mechanisms
- The FRCDesignApp part library

### Onshape Learning Center

[Onshape Learning Center](https://learn.onshape.com): Onshape's official courses, for when you want more depth on a specific tool.

## Calculators

- [ReCalc](https://www.reca.lc): calculators for mechanism speed and torque, belt/chain centre distance, and more. Use it for every mechanism.
- [AMBCalc](https://ambcalc.com): more mechanism calculators

## Game Manual

The [Game Manual](https://www.firstinspires.org/resource-library/frc/competition-manual-qa-system) has the robot rules (frame perimeter, weight, height and extension limits, bumpers). Read the robot construction section before starting CAD. See [FRC Robot Rules Basics](./frc-robot-rules) for an overview.

## Other

- [Project B Mechanism Encyclopaedia](https://www.projectb.net.au/resources/robot-mechanisms/): explains common robot mechanisms and when to use them
- **Vendor example designs:** WCP, REV and AndyMark publish tested designs with CAD, such as elevator kits and the Everybot
- **Technical binders:** many top teams publish one for each robot, explaining every mechanism and why they chose it. Find them on Chief Delphi, usually in [Robot Showcase](https://www.chiefdelphi.com/c/first/robot-showcase/37).
- **Robot in 3 Days (Ri3D):** after kickoff, several teams each build a robot for the new game in 3 days and post videos, showing quickly what ideas work. Search for Ri3D on youtube.
- [Behind the Bumpers](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLkZ6_Ld1x9Y_GDGwzzxD_vi_5tnlmAWJo): short videos from the [FUN (FIRST Updates Now)](https://www.youtube.com/@FUNRoboticsNetwork) YouTube channel where teams walk through their robots at competitions
- [The Blue Alliance](https://www.thebluealliance.com): match videos and robot photos for any team or event
- [Team 971](https://www.frc971.org): known for their advanced custom mechanisms and design talks
